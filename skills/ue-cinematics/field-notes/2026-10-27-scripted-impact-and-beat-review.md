What the reviewer rejected, and what to plan in from the start:

- **An impact is layers, not one emitter.** A spark burst alone read as "liquid". Plan: sparks + solid debris
  (broken pieces of the thing that breaks - shackle/chain fragments as physics-free meshes flung along the blow) +
  ground dust + a short camera shake / hit-stop. If the broken object visibly survives intact, the break is not believed.
- **Particles meant for ground impacts vanish on a body.** A ground-slam dust system spawned at a character's chest
  barely showed in a tight shot. Check every effect at the shot's real framing before calling it done.
- **Restate the beat order back before building when the wording is ambiguous.** "When she gets up she should shake
  herself" was read as "shake while standing"; the intent (said a round earlier) was "rises, lifts into the air, and
  only then shakes with a sharp spin". One line - "rises → hovers → spins the dust off, right?" - saves a round.
- **The rest of the frame counts.** Bystanders standing in the hero's sightline and not looking where he looks read as
  unnatural and block the shot: give them a step aside and a look toward the action.
- **Review the moment, not around it.** Screenshot batches with gaps missed the 0.6 s shake twice; slow the world to
  ~0.04 exactly over the beat (trigger on the beat's own state, not on the log - the log file is flushed late) and
  shoot continuously through it.
